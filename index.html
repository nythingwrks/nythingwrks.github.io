about

Nell Lang (she/her) is a multi-medium creative & occasional live-coder. She has a BSc(hons) in Psychology from the University of St Andrews and a Level 1 qualification in British Sign Language. Her interests include: music and sound design, social models of mental health, storytelling, and improv comedy.

Nell is currently a volunteer Ambassador at Nightstop, a part of the Roundhouse Music Collective 25/26, and a proud member of Peckham Digital's Youth Advisory Board.

She is always looking for new ways to expand her pleasingly odd and assorted skill set, and is getting good at writing about herself in the third person.
